LIFT RISES, THEN IT BREAKS.

Lift grows in a straight line with angle until the stall angle, then falls away. Drag keeps climbing with CL squared, so the best glide sits well before stall.

Drag the marker on either curve to change the angle of attack. Arrow keys work too.

A SIMPLE MODEL, STATED PLAINLY.

Lift and drag come from the dynamic pressure, the wing area and two coefficients that depend only on the angle of attack.

    Educational approximation only. Lift slope is approximated as 2π per radian, with a heuristic stall loss beyond 15°. Drag uses a simple quadratic polar. Streamlines are illustrative, not CFD.
